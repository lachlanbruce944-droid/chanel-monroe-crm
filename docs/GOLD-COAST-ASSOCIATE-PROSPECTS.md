## Publication
1. Chanel opens a prospect's Instagram/site and verifies the profile, latest work, photo and contact details.
2. Record outreach by selecting Contacted. After an actual connection is established, select Connected.
3. The owner clicks Publish to website and confirms that the person agrees to be listed.
4. A database gate requires an active Associate, recorded outreach and Connected status. Owner access is required to publish.
5. The public Associates section is empty/hidden until at least one qualifying profile is explicitly published. Mock cards have been removed. Changing the relationship away from Connected automatically hides the profile.
6. Hide from website unpublishes immediately in the database; refresh an already-open website to reload its public list.
